Local Business Website & Live Pitch Project
Project Overview
This project involves designing and developing a professional website for a real local business such as a restaurant, café, gym, salon, boutique, clinic, or any small business. The purpose of the website is to improve the business's online presence, attract new customers, and provide important business information in a professional manner.
Additionally, a short business pitch is prepared to explain how the website can help the business grow and improve customer engagement---
 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to:
- Create a professional business website
- Improve the business's online visibility
- Provide customers with easy access to services and contact information
- Demonstrate real-world web development skills
- Practice client communication and business pitching
